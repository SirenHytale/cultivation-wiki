Glossary

Cultivation borrows its vocabulary from Xianxia — Chinese immortal-hero fiction — and then gives several of those words a specific mechanical meaning. This is what each one means here.

The Path

TermMeaning
Qi 氣 Spirit energy, and the currency of all progression. Every advancement and breakthrough is bought with banked Qi.
Cultivation 修仙 The practice of refining Qi to climb toward immortality. Literally "cultivating immortality".
Realm One of the seven great tiers of power, from Body Refinement to Void Refinement. See Realms & Stages.
Sub-stage The four rungs inside each realm — Early, Middle, Late, Peak. Twenty-eight rungs in total.
Advancement The short ritual that moves you one sub-stage. Needs 200 chunk Qi and 8 seconds at the bottom of the ladder.
Breakthrough The far bigger ritual that carries you from Peak into the next realm. Needs 750 chunk Qi, 24+ seconds, and draws tribulation lightning.
Tribulation 天劫 The heavens' answer to a breakthrough: lightning that lands every 6 seconds of ritual progress, and can kill you.
Heart-Devil Trial 心魔劫 What replaces lightning for a cultivator leaned far into Yin or Yang. It attacks composure rather than health.
Composure 道心 The pool the Heart-Devil drains. Armed fresh each attempt; if it empties, the ritual fails.
Qi Deviation 走火入魔 What happens when composure breaks — the ritual fails and you are demoted a sub-stage.
Karma A ledger written by the cultivators you kill. The heavens collect it at your next tribulation, as deeper and more frequent bolts.
Aura 氣息 The visible Qi a cultivator gives off — realm sets its color and density, sub-stage its size. A second, inward-converging aura marks a fruitful meditation. See Auras.
Title 稱號 An earned, purely cosmetic epithet worn above your head, in chat and on the boards. See Titles.
Profile 存檔 One of up to three separate cultivators kept on one account. Realm, race, dao, skills and arts travel with it; sect, abode, formations and marriage stay with the account. See Profiles.

Qi & the Land

TermMeaning
Meditation Sitting cross-legged to draw ambient Qi from the chunk beneath you. Move more than 0.35 blocks and any ritual in progress is forfeit.
Spirit Vein A real per-chunk pool of Qi that depletes as you draw on it and regenerates over time. Seeded deterministically from the world seed.
Rich vein A chunk that rolled a larger pool — roughly 3% of chunks. Regenerates ×1.25. The minimum ground a sect hall can be claimed on.
Dragon vein The rarest tier, roughly 0.3% of chunks. Regenerates ×1.6. The land people fight sect wars over.
Cultivation core Spirit, Profound or Divine — dropped by creatures, absorbed for an instant lump of Qi. At most one per kill.
Qi Gathering Talisman A carried item that raises your vein absorption rate while meditating. It only needs to be in your hotbar.
Spirit Stone The upkeep currency. Drops from kills and from surviving tribulation bolts; pays for a Cave Abode.
Weather resonance A meditation multiplier from active weather — ×1.5 when it matches your dao's element, ×1.15 otherwise.

The Dao

TermMeaning
Dao 道 "The Way." Mechanically, the page holding your elemental affinity and your Yin-Yang balance.
Wu Xing 五行 The five classical elements — Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth — arranged in a ring where each overcomes the next.
Shadow ring The five darker elements forming a second counter-cycle. Cross-ring matchups are neutral.
Yin-Yang balance A drifting axis written by your deeds, the ground you meditate on, and your race's bias. Sitting near the middle pays a bonus to Qi and damage.
Lean How far from the middle your balance sits, as a 0–1 fraction. At 0.5 you face the Heart-Devil instead of lightning.
Devil Path 魔道 The formal path of a deeply Yin cultivator, with its own combat perks and the ability to harvest Qi from players you kill.
Righteous Path 正道 The Yang counterpart, with its own edge against the Devil Path.

Arts & Treasures

TermMeaning
Technique / Art An active ability — sword flight, a thunder palm, a dash — gated behind a realm, a Qi cost and a cooldown.
Manual A lootable book that teaches a technique or a skill-tree node. Where Requires-Manual is on, the realm alone is not enough.
Skill tree A nine-branch radial tree of 117 nodes. Points come from every rank-up: +1 per advancement, +3 per breakthrough.
Transcendence The skill tree's eighth tier — dual-prerequisite capstone nodes at the ends of the branches.
Life-Bound Treasure A weapon or armour piece bound to you personally, which levels up from use. Weapons gain XP from hits landed, armour from hits survived.
Alchemy 丹 Brewing pills from cultivation cores — wards against tribulation and the inner demon, and aids to a stubborn breakthrough.
Refinement 煉器 Tempering a weapon in tribulation lightning to give it a Dao element and a tier from I to IX. A failed tempering can shatter the blade.
Spirit beast 靈獸 A tamed or hatched companion that cultivates its own realm beside you, as a Guardian, Warden or Spirit-Gatherer.

Society

TermMeaning
Sect 宗 A player guild with Elders, join policies and a motto. Capped at 12 members by default.
Sect hall A sect's claimed chunk, which must sit on a rich vein or better. It powers the sect-wide Qi bonus — a sect with no hall gets nothing.
Inscription A technique manual carved into a hall, lending that art to every member for as long as the sect holds the hall.
Hall beacon The standing column of light over a claimed hall, visible to anyone. Defaults to the vein's own color unless a banner flies.
Banner 旗幟 The color a sect flies over its hall — the Four Symbols, Golden Court or Jade Terrace — chosen by the leader or an elder, and tinting the hall's map pin. See Sects.
Formation 陣 A placed, chunk-anchored array — amplifying allied meditation, choking outsiders' Qi, or rooting and wounding trespassers.
Cave Abode 洞府 A personal claimed dwelling whose Spirit Spring accrues Qi in real time, online or not. Collected on site.
Spirit Spring The Abode's pool. Fills every real-world hour up to a cap that grows with your realm.
Seclusion Retreating into an Abode to convert time into Qi, capped at 24 hours per retreat.
Sect war A declared siege to take a rival sect's hall — hold the ground long enough inside the window and the vein is yours.
Duel A consensual fight for a wager of banked Qi. Works even on worlds with PvP switched off.
A note on the Chinese The characters shown are the terms Xianxia fiction conventionally uses, included because the mod is fully localised in Simplified Chinese. You never need to read or type them to play.
