Commands

The mod registers two root commands: /cultivation (aliases: /cult, /qi) and /sect.

Arguments in {braces} are required, arguments in [brackets] are optional. Anything that takes a player takes a player reference - the name of an online player.

The whole sect suite is mounted underneath the cultivation root as well, so /cultivation sect create and /sect create are the same command acting on the same data. The shorter /sect spelling is used throughout this page.

Profiles

/cultivation profile manages your saved cultivators - see Profiles. Numbers refer to the list's 1-based slots; names match case-insensitively.

CommandDescriptionPermission
/cultivation profile [list]Lists your profiles with realm, stage and Qi, marking the one you are on.cultivation
/cultivation profile new confirm (create)Starts a brand-new cultivator from Body Refinement and switches you onto it. Only acts when passed the literal word confirm.cultivation
/cultivation profile switch {number|name} (use, load)Saves the cultivator you are on and loads that one.cultivation
/cultivation profile rename {name}Renames the profile you are on. Names are capped at 24 characters.cultivation
/cultivation profile delete {number|name} (remove)Erases that save for good. Refuses the profile you are on and the last one you have. The Profiles menu asks twice before doing this; the command acts at once.cultivation

The test-profile sandbox is a separate, operator-granted toolkit on the same command - see Profiles for what it exempts you from. Every branch below except test itself also requires that the sandbox is the profile you are currently on.

CommandDescriptionPermission
/cultivation profile test {duration}Enters (or extends) the sandbox cultivator. The duration is required - 90s, 30m, 4h, 2d, or a bare number of minutes; 1 minute to 30 days. It is wall-clock, so logging off does not pause it.cultivation.profile.test
/cultivation profile realm {realm}Sets the sandbox's realm by enum name, e.g. VOID_REFINEMENT.cultivation.profile.test
/cultivation profile stage {stage}Sets the sub-stage: EARLY, MIDDLE, LATE or PEAK.cultivation.profile.test
/cultivation profile qi {amount}Sets banked Qi.cultivation.profile.test
/cultivation profile points {amount}Sets (not adds) available skill points.cultivation.profile.test
/cultivation profile race {race}Sets the sandbox's race, bypassing the realm gate.cultivation.profile.test
/cultivation profile resetWipes the sandbox back to a brand-new cultivator without leaving it.cultivation.profile.test

Spirit Beasts

Binding a beast happens out in the world rather than through a command - see Spirit Beasts.

CommandDescriptionPermission
/cultivation beast (companion, pet)Prints your bound beast's species, kind, element, realm, and current contribution.cultivation
/cultivation beast summon (call)Calls your bound beast into the world at your side. Its bonus applies only while it is summoned.cultivation
/cultivation beast dismiss (recall)Sends the beast's body away. It keeps every stage it has cultivated.cultivation
/cultivation beast feedFeeds the entire stack in your hand to the beast, converting each item's Qi worth into beast XP.cultivation
/cultivation beast infoThe same readout as bare /cultivation beast, in subcommand form — species, kind, element, realm and contribution, plus the arts it knows, what it would evolve into and whether it can carry you.cultivation
/cultivation beast art [use <name>]Commands one of the beast’s arts. Run bare to list what it knows and what is still ahead of it.cultivation
/cultivation beast evolveGrows the beast into its next form, carrying its levels and bond across. If it cannot yet, this says exactly what is missing.cultivation
/cultivation beast mountSummons the beast in its rideable body, for the seven species that grow large enough.cultivation
/cultivation beast release [confirm]Severs the bond entirely so you can tame another. Everything the beast cultivated is lost, so it only acts when passed the literal word confirm.cultivation

Duels

/cultivation duel takes an action word, and some actions take a further argument. See Duels.

CommandDescriptionPermission
/cultivation duel challenge {player} [wager]Stakes banked Qi and challenges another cultivator. Omitting the wager duels for nothing; a wager that is not a whole number is rejected by the engine, and a negative one is refused outright.cultivation
/cultivation duel accept {player}Accepts that player's pending challenge, starting the duel.cultivation
/cultivation duel decline {player}Declines that player's pending challenge.cultivation
/cultivation duel yield (forfeit)Forfeits your current duel - the wager goes to your opponent.cultivation

Sects

Every command here is also reachable as /cultivation sect .... In-sect authority (leader, elder, member) is enforced per action rather than by permission node - see Sects.

CommandDescriptionPermission
/sectOpens the sect menu UI.cultivation.sect
/sect create {name}Founds a new sect under that name.cultivation.sect
/sect invite {player}Invites an online player to your sect.cultivation.sect
/sect join {sect}Joins a sect by name.cultivation.sect
/sect leaveLeaves your current sect.cultivation.sect
/sect kick {player}Removes a member from your sect, whether they are online or not.cultivation.sect
/sect disband [confirm]Warns, naming the sect and its member count; confirm dissolves it entirely and tells every member.cultivation.sect
/sect claimClaims (or moves) the sect hall to the chunk you are standing in. Only succeeds on a Spirit Vein of the configured quality.cultivation.sect
/sect inscribeCarves the technique manual in your hand into the hall, teaching that art to every member for as long as the sect holds the hall. The manual is consumed. Run empty-handed, it scours the existing inscription away instead.cultivation.sect
/sect banner [set <banner>]With no argument, lists the banners your sect may fly, marking the current one. With a name - azure, or the full cultivation:azure - flies it over the hall; none returns to the plain vein light. Leader or elder only.cultivation.sect
/sect dao [element]Names the sect’s own Dao, so ground it holds turns Qi toward that element. Run bare to see the current one, or pass none to clear it. Leader only.cultivation.sect
/sect building [claim|dao|abandon|list]Raises a holding on the chunk you are standing in, toggles whether that building’s ground carries the sect Dao, gives one up, or lists what the sect holds and what it could still raise. Leader only to claim or change.cultivation.sect
/sect info (i)Prints your sect's standing - members, hall, banner, and bonuses.cultivation.sect
/sect menu (ui)Opens the sect menu UI, the same page bare /sect opens.cultivation.sect

Admin Commands

Every command below that takes a [player] works on a name the server has actually seen before. Aim one at somebody who has never joined this world - a typo, usually - and it says so rather than reporting nothing at all.

CommandDescriptionPermission
/cultivation adminOpens the live in-game config editor UI.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin setrealm {realm} | player {realm} {name}Sets a player's realm directly, keeping their current stage.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin setstage {stage} | player {stage} {name}Sets a player's sub-stage directly (Early, Middle, Late, or Peak), keeping their current realm.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin setqi {qi} | player {qi} {name}Sets a player's currently banked Qi directly.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin setrace {race} | player {race} {name}Sets a player's race directly. Bypasses that race's unlock realm unless Race-Admin-Bypasses-Realm-Gate is set to false.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin grantracechoice [amount] | player {amount} {name}Grants additional race changes, letting the player reopen the race menu and pick again. Defaults to 1.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin grantskillpoints [amount] | player {amount} {name}Grants additional skill tree points. Defaults to 1.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin setlifebound {level} [xp] | player {level} [xp] {name}Force-sets the level, and optionally the banked XP within that level, of the Life-Bound Treasure the player is holding.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin test grant {duration} {name}Puts another player into a sandbox profile for a set time, or extends the one they have.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin test realm {realm} {name}Sets that sandbox cultivator's realm. Refused unless they are on the sandbox slot.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin test stage {stage} {name}Sets that sandbox cultivator's stage.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin test qi {amount} {name}Sets that sandbox cultivator's banked Qi.cultivation.admin
/cultivation admin test end {name}Ends a sandbox profile early and returns the player to their own progress.cultivation.admin
/landNames the claim you are standing on and opens its land rules.cultivation
/cultivation admin reset | reset player {name}Resets a player's cultivation progress back to Body Refinement, Early-Stage, with 0 Qi.cultivation.admin
